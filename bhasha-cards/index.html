Questions

Are the words right for my family?

Starter decks use the most common words, but every family is different. Some say Ammi, some Maa, some Mummy. Every card is fully editable.

Where are my photos stored?

Only on your device, inside the browser's storage. Nothing is uploaded and there's no account to create.

What age is it for?

Ages 1–7 for the picture cards. Older kids enjoy making their own decks.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once it's open, you can install it to your home screen and use it without internet.
