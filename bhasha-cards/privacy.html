Bhasha Cards

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

Bhasha Cards is a web app for making flash cards in your family's language, with photos of real relatives. It's made by Khola Labs, a one-person studio run by Narender Kumar ("I", "me"). Family photos are personal, so the app was built to keep them on your device. This page explains how.

Short version: your photos and cards never leave your device. There are no accounts and no uploads, and I have no server that stores your data.

Your photos and cards stay on your device

  • When you add a photo, the app shrinks and crops it inside your browser and saves it in your browser's local database (IndexedDB) on that device. It is never uploaded.
  • Your decks, card words, chosen language and print settings are saved in your browser's local storage on that device.
  • The app works offline. It saves its own files (code, styles, icons) in your browser's cache so it can load without a connection. That cache doesn't contain your photos.
  • Because nothing is synced, your cards exist only in the browser where you made them. I can't see, recover or restore them.

No accounts, no tracking

There's no sign-up or login. The app has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking scripts.

Read-aloud (text-to-speech)

When you tap to hear a word, the app uses your browser's or operating system's built-in speech feature. Many voices run entirely on your device. However, some browsers offer online voices (for example, the "Google" voices in Chrome), and these may send the word being spoken to the browser vendor to generate the audio. That happens under the browser vendor's privacy policy, not mine. Only the card's word is involved, never your photos. To keep speech fully on-device, pick a local voice in your system settings or use a browser that doesn't use online voices.

Fonts and hosting

The app loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers to download them. Like any website, the host serving the app's pages receives standard technical data such as your IP address and browser type. I don't use that data to identify you.

Payments (when Pro launches)

Pro will be a one-time purchase handled by Gumroad, which acts as the seller of record. Gumroad collects your name, email and payment details under Gumroad's privacy policy. Gumroad tells me about the sale (including the email you used) so I can provide support. The app never sees your card details. To unlock Pro, the app checks your licence key with Gumroad's licence-verification service. Only the key is sent, and the result is saved on your device.

Launch waitlist (optional)

If you choose to give your email on the waitlist form, I'll use it only to tell you when Pro launches. It won't be sold, shared for marketing, or used for anything else. You can ask me to remove it at any time. If I use a mailing-list service to send that email, it will hold your address for that purpose only.

Children

Bhasha Cards is meant for parents and family members to set up and use with their children. Children may look at and listen to the cards, but the app collects no personal information from anyone: everything stays on the device. Adults should be the ones adding photos and making purchases.

Deleting your data

To delete everything, remove decks and photos in the app, or clear this site's data in your browser settings (for example, Site settings → Clear data). Uninstalling the home-screen app also removes it on most devices. To be taken off the waitlist, or for questions about a purchase, email me.

Changes

If the way the app handles data changes, I'll update this page and its effective date.
