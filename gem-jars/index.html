Gem Jars

A calm sort puzzle · Coming soon to Google Play

Tap a jar to lift its top gem, then tap another jar to drop it. A gem can only land on the same colour or in an empty jar. Once every jar holds a single colour, the level is solved.

What's in it

  • Endless levels that ramp slowly from 3 up to 10 colours. A built-in solver checks that every level can be solved.
  • Daily Jar: one shared puzzle a day, a streak counter, and a spoiler-free result you can share.
  • No timer, no pressure. Undo, hints and an extra jar are there when you're stuck.
  • A colour-blind symbol mode, plus sound and haptics you can turn off.
  • An optional evening reminder for the Daily Jar. It's off until you turn it on.

Gem Jars is free and supported by ads. Most ads are optional: you choose to watch one for a hint or double gems. A short ad appears now and then between levels, and never during the Daily Jar.

Privacy

Gem Jars has no accounts, and your progress stays on your phone. Ads come from Google AdMob. Read the privacy policy.
