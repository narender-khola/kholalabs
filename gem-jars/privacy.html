Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 October 2026

Gem Jars is a puzzle game for Android made by Khola Labs, a one-person studio run by Narender Kumar ("I", "me"). This page explains what data the game handles, in plain language. Short version: the game itself collects nothing about you. It has no accounts and no servers of its own. The only data that leaves your phone goes to Google's ad service, which shows the ads that keep the game free.

What stays on your phone

Your level, stars, gems, Daily Jar streak, unlocked styles and settings (sound, haptics, symbol mode, reminders) are saved in the app's local storage on your device. I don't receive them, and the game doesn't send them anywhere.

If Android's own backup is turned on, Android may include this app data in your device backup to your Google account. That backup is handled by Google under your account settings. I can't access it.

Ads (Google AdMob)

Gem Jars shows ads through Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK. Most ads are ones you choose to watch, for example to get a hint or double your gems. A short ad also appears occasionally between levels.

To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share with Google:

  • your device's advertising ID (or other device identifiers),
  • device and network information, such as model, operating system, language and IP address (which can show your approximate location),
  • ad interaction data, such as which ads were shown or tapped, along with diagnostic and performance information.

Google handles this data under its own policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. I don't receive personal information from AdMob, only aggregate reports such as how many ads were shown.

Your choices: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).

EEA, UK and Switzerland: in these regions the game shows Google's consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) before personalised ads are used, so you can accept or decline. If you decline, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which use limited data for things like frequency capping and fraud prevention.

Reminders (optional)

You can turn on a daily evening reminder for the Daily Jar. It's off by default, and Android asks for your permission first. Reminders are scheduled locally on your phone with Android's notification system. No server is involved, and nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in Android's notification settings.

Other device features

  • Haptics: the game uses your phone's vibration for small taps of feedback. You can turn this off in settings. No data is involved.
  • Sharing: if you tap Share after a Daily Jar, the game hands a short result text (for example, puzzle number, moves and streak) to the share sheet, and you pick where it goes. Nothing is shared unless you do this.
  • Internet access is used only to load ads.

What I don't do

  • No accounts, logins or sign-up.
  • No analytics or tracking of my own, and no servers of my own.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location.
  • I don't sell your data.

Children

Gem Jars is a general-audience game. It isn't directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child's data has reached me, email me and I'll help.

Deleting your data

Everything the game stores is on your device. To delete it, use "Reset progress" in the game's settings, clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the game. I don't hold an account or any data about you that could be deleted on my side. For data that Google collected through ads, use your Google account and device ad settings, or see Google's policy linked above.

Changes

If the game starts handling data differently (for example, if in-app purchases are added through Google Play), I'll update this page and change the effective date above.
